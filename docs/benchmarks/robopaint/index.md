# RoboPaint tasks

Our own benchmark: a Franka Panda with a brush writes or paints the exemplar it is shown —
lettering, kaishu and xingshu calligraphy, acrylic and oil — and the verifier checks both the sheet
and the strokes (no colouring in; [process rules](about.md#the-process-rules)). The line drawings of
the first version are excluded and stay greyed at the bottom, for the record.
