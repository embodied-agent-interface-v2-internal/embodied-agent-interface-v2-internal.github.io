# KinDER tasks

objects that we keep of KinDER's 31 families, and, greyed at the bottom, the 6 others we kept until
2026-10-04 (two of them in PyBullet) and one fixture. KinDER publishes no
per-task demonstration, so every row shows the starting scene.
