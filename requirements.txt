# Documentation toolchain. Pinned exactly on purpose.

# MkDocs is mid-way through a contested v2 transition (Material prints a
# warning about it on every build). Exact pins mean a contributor who clones
# this repo next month gets the same site you do. Bump them deliberately, in
# their own PR, with a build to prove it still works.

# Generates the gallery, triage board and coverage pages at build time.
